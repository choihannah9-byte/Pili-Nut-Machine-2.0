Rev D lays the machine out as a linear gravity cascade (hopper and grading at the right, rollers in the middle, separation and trays at the left) so every hand-off is supported and visible. Nuts are fed tip-first with the ridge in the V-channel groove, so the rollers load ridge-to-face across the width, as in the 8 Sept verification note. Separation follows that note too: coarse screen, then the 8 mm screen, then cross-flow air on chips, then the sorting tray.
